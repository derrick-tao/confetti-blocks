Privacy & local storage

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Confetti Blocks is maintained by derrick-tao.

What the game stores on your device

This app saves your best score and sound, volume, and calm-mode preferences in this browser’s localStorage. It caches game files for offline play. It does not save an in-progress board. Your browser can clear or evict this data; clearing site data removes the downloaded game, scores, and preferences.

What the app sends

The app does not upload scores or gameplay, ask for an account, name, location, or contact details, or include ads, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime scripts. It requests its own game files and checks for updates. Sounds are generated on your device.

Hosting security logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages’ disclosure and GitHub’s privacy statement for the provider’s practices. These external documents require an internet connection; the game and this notice remain available offline after downloading.

Children and families

The game has no chat, accounts, advertising, purchases, or public sharing. Parents and guardians can contact the maintainer through the repository’s issue tracker or maintainer profile. Please do not post children’s names, contact details, identifiers, or other personal information in public issues. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact the maintainer.

This notice describes the current app. It is not a certification of legal compliance. Target-market children’s privacy obligations and hosting arrangements still require review before child-directed promotion. Any future accounts, analytics, advertising, or sharing would require a new review and updated notice.

Your controls

Sound, volume, and calm mode are available in the game settings. Use the browser’s site-data controls to remove local preferences and cached files. Reopen the game online to restore offline files. Other project sites on the same github.io origin may share browser storage; a dedicated hosting origin is a recommended future isolation measure.
