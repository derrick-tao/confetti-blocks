A FEW LITTLE POINTERS

Let's make things pop.

Fit pieces onto the 8×8 board. Complete a row or column to clear it. Use all three pieces to get a fresh set. The game ends when none of your remaining pieces fit.

No timerNo rotationJust possibilities

Your moves

Touch or mouse: drag a piece, or select a piece and tap its top-left destination on the board.

Keyboard: 1, 2, 3 selects a piece. Arrows move the preview. Enter or Space places it. Escape deselects.

Your points

Each block is worth 10 points. A clear adds 100 × lines² × combo streak. Clear on consecutive moves to grow your combo. Clear the whole board for 500 extra points!

Best score is saved in this browser. No sign-in, ads, purchases, or tracking.
